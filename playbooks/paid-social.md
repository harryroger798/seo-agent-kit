# Paid social

Paid social is an offer, an audience, a creative system, and a measurement model. The ad is the smallest part.

## Campaign architecture

| Layer | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | WhatsApp conversations, leads, or sales | Matches buying behaviour |
| Audience | Country, age, interests, lookalike, retargeting | Broad enough to learn |
| Offer | What is sold, price, delivery, guarantee | The differentiator |
| Creative | 5 images + 1 video | Tests category demand |
| Destination | WhatsApp or landing page | Low friction, qualifies |
| Measurement | Cost per conversation, close rate, AOV, ROAS | Conversations lead |

## The five creative categories

1. Product category A — hook, proof, delivery.
2. Product category B — hook, proof, compatibility.
3. Product category C — hook, proof, region.
4. Add-on / booster — hook, proof, safety.
5. Subscription — hook, proof, term and renewal.

## Ad copy formulas

| Formula | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Price shock | Official price → your price → proof | “The game at ₹4,999? We deliver the key for less.” |
| Problem → solution | “Tired of…” → “Here is…” → CTA | “Tired of paying full price for one game?” |
| Proof first | Customer result → offer → CTA | “He got his key in 4 minutes.” |
| Objection | “Is it genuine?” → answer → CTA | “Every key is checked. If it fails, we replace it.” |

## The WhatsApp flow

1. Greeting: which game or subscription?
2. Qualify: platform, region, title, edition.
3. Quote: price, delivery, validity, replacement.
4. Proof: delivery screenshot, no personal data.
5. Close: payment link or UPI.
6. Deliver: key + activation steps.
7. Follow-up: one message after 7 days, only if opted in.

## Measurement

| Metric | What it tells you | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per conversation | Creative/audience efficiency | Kill ads above ceiling after 3 days |
| Qualified rate | Message quality | Below 30% → tighten copy |
| Close rate | Sales flow | Below 15% → fix quote/proof/payment |
| AOV | Bundle effect | Add a second item |
| ROAS | Economics | Below 2 → rework offer |
| Refund rate | Fulfilment quality | Above 5% → stop scaling |

## Compliance

- Only advertise products you are authorised to sell.
- State region, platform, delivery, validity.
- No “official” or publisher logo to imply endorsement.
- No fake countdowns, “only 2 left,” or fake reviews.
- WhatsApp opt-in must be explicit.
- Honour the replacement/refund policy.

## Weekly cadence

- Monday: cost per conversation by creative.
- Tuesday: pause bottom 20%, duplicate top 20%.
- Wednesday: test one new hook.
- Thursday: review qualification and close rate.
- Friday: add one customer proof asset.
- Weekly: policy and delivery check.
- Monthly: refresh the video and offer.
