# AI search

AI search is a retrieval layer on top of the same web. The winners are crawlable, specific, corroborated, and worth quoting.

## The citation funnel

| Stage | What must be true | What fails |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Crawler can fetch | Blocked, paywalled, JS-only |
| Index | Indexed and snippet-eligible | Noindex, canonical, thin |
| Retrieval | Matches the query and fan-out | Exact-match only |
| Grounding | Clear, verifiable passage | Vague, buried, unsourced |
| Citation | Engine trusts the source | No corroboration |
| Referral | User clicks | Weak title, poor page |

## Engine taste

| Engine | Observed preference | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Google AI Overviews | Broad citer; Reddit, YouTube, publishers | Rank, be quotable, cover fan-out |
| Google AI Mode | Conversational; YouTube, Reddit, Facebook | Answer follow-ups; video/community |
| ChatGPT | Wikipedia, Reddit, Forbes; listicles | Entity clarity, listicle inclusion |
| Perplexity | Citation-forward; concentrated | Be in the top citation set |
| Gemini / Claude / Copilot | Varies by integration | Test each engine |

## Eight tactics

1. Entity clarity: Organization schema, NAP, About, sameAs.
2. Answer-first pages: answer in the first 60 words.
3. Statistics with sources and dates.
4. Named quotations from real practitioners.
5. Comparison and alternatives pages.
6. Original data or experience.
7. Corroboration: Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, reviews.
8. Freshness: visible update dates, re-verified facts.

## Five things to stop paying for

- llms.txt as a ranking play.
- Fake mentions.
- Schema theatre.
- Mass fan-out pages.
- “Chunking” for AI.

## The 30-day sprint

| Week | Work | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit 10 money pages; fix indexation and answer-first | Scorecard + 3 fixes |
| 2 | Add statistics, sources, quotations to top 3 | 3 evidence-dense pages |
| 3 | Publish one comparison + one data asset | 2 citation assets |
| 4 | Earn corroboration: 10 community answers, 2 videos, 5 reviews | Presence outside your domain |

## The interrogation loop

After publishing, test the brand:

1. Ask ?best [category] in [city]? in Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot.
2. If the brand is missing or at the bottom, ask ?Why didn?t you include [brand]?? and ?Where is [brand]??
3. Ask ?What sources would change your answer??
4. Fix the cited sources: directories, Reddit, YouTube, reviews, comparison pages, government data.
5. Re-test in 7 days.

Do not argue. Ask for the sources; fix the sources.

## The India citation stack

After Google Business Profile, claim and complete:

1. Justdial
2. Sulekha
3. IndiaMART (if B2B)
4. Google, Bing, Apple, Facebook, and the website
5. Industry and local directories with real editorial standards

These are frequently cited by AI answers in India. Keep NAP identical.

## Measurement

Three layers:

1. **Retrieval** — GSC generative AI impressions.
2. **Citation** — a fixed 30-prompt panel.
3. **Outcome** — GA4 AI Assistant, branded search, assisted conversions.

Never collapse them into one score without a formula.
