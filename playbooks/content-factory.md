# The content factory

One keyword → one canonical article → seven derivatives. The factory is a sequence of gates, not a content calendar.

## The eight stages

| # | Stage | Input | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand | GSC, keyword tool, sales objections | One money query + fan-out | Intent is clear |
| 2 | SERP delta | Top 10, AI Overview, Reddit/YouTube | What exists, what is missing | Gap is real |
| 3 | Silo | Money query, product, proof | 5 silos, 3–5 subtopics each | One intent per page |
| 4 | Brief | Silo row, proof, links | One-page brief | A second person can explain it |
| 5 | Draft | Brief, sources, product facts | HTML article | Every claim sourced |
| 6 | Edit | Draft, AI-tell list | Tighter, proof-dense article | No invented facts |
| 7 | Derive | Canonical article | Medium, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, email | Hook changes, facts don't |
| 8 | Measure | GSC, GA4, AI report | One-page result | Metric chosen before publish |

## The 48-hour calendar

| Block | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 AM | Demand, SERP delta, silo | One row |
| Day 1 PM | Brief, proof, links | Brief |
| Day 2 AM | Draft | HTML |
| Day 2 PM | Edit, fact-check, schema, images | Publish-ready |
| Day 3 AM | Three core derivatives | Social drafts |
| Day 3 PM | Publish, index, link, track | Live URL + baseline |

## The seven derivatives

| Asset | What changes | What must not change |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | Longer intro, narrative | Facts, links, claim |
| LinkedIn | Professional framing, bullets | Numbers, sources |
| X thread | One idea per post | Numbers, sources, link |
| Facebook | Local/community angle | Price, availability |
| Instagram | Carousel, visual proof | Claims, product |
| YouTube | Demo, chapters, transcript | Steps, safety, result |
| Email | One story, one lesson | Offer, deadline, terms |

## The derivative rule

A derivative may change the hook, the format, and the length. It may not change the facts, the price, the links, or the claim. If a derivative needs a new fact, the canonical article is incomplete. Fix the source first.

## The media layer

The article is not finished when the text is done. Add:

| Asset | Rule |
|---|---|
| Featured image | 1200?630, OG/social/blog card. **Not the first body image.** |
| In-body images | 2?4 different images: after intro, in a step, near comparison, before FAQ. |
| FAQ | 5?7 real questions, 40?60 word answers, visible. |
| Video | One relevant video, transcript, VideoObject schema. |
| Citations | 2?3 primary sources, inline, tied to a claim. |
| Internal links | 3?5 contextual links, including the money page. |
| Key takeaways | Three bullets for skimmers and AI extraction. |
| Author/date | Visible author, published, last updated. |

Use `skills/blog-media/SKILL.md` and `prompts/13-blog-media.md`.

## The QA gate

- [ ] First 60 words answer the query.
- [ ] Every number has a source and a date.
- [ ] Every quote has a real name.
- [ ] No invented experience.
- [ ] One table or decision aid.
- [ ] 3–5 internal links.
- [ ] 2–3 primary external sources.
- [ ] Product URL once.
- [ ] Author, date, correction path.
- [ ] Metric chosen.

## Common failures

- **The brief is an outline copied from the SERP.** Add one section no competitor has.
- **The draft is comprehensive but not quotable.** Add one sentence with a number, a source, and a date.
- **The social post invents a number.** Fix the canonical article, then derive again.
- **The page has no proof.** Do not publish; go get the proof.
