## Compliance

- [ ] Authorised to sell the key
- [ ] Region and platform stated
- [ ] Delivery and validity accurate
- [ ] No “official” claim without proof
- [ ] No fake discount or scarcity
- [ ] Replacement policy honoured
- [ ] Support path live

## What not to do

- Do not claim “official” or use the publisher’s logo to imply endorsement.
- Do not sell keys in violation of the platform’s terms.
- Do not use “lifetime” for a subscription.
- Do not invent reviews or “only 2 left.”
