## What is already strong

- Deep local schema: TravelAgency, Place, OpeningHours, GeoCoordinates.
- 372 URLs: real service and route coverage.
- 3,364 words on the homepage: enough to answer local questions.
- Explicit AI crawler allowances in robots.txt.

## What to fix first

1. **Performance.** 598.6 KB HTML and 160 scripts is the constraint. Set a budget: HTML <150 KB, JS <250 KB, TTFB <800 ms.
2. **Image alt text.** 13 images without alt. Fix fleet, destination, and route images.
3. **GBP cadence.** Weekly post, 20+ real photos, Q&A seeded, review request at the moment of delight.
4. **Area pages.** Each area page needs local proof: route, travel time, local review, photo, FAQ. No city-swapped doorway pages.
5. **Review engine.** Ask after every completed trip; respond within 48 hours.

## The 2026 weights

Whitespark’s 2026 survey: GBP signals ~32% of the local pack, on-page ~33% of local organic, reviews ~16–20%. Primary category ranks first, followed by proximity and title keywords. Use these to prioritise, not to promise a result.

## What not to do

- Do not keyword-stuff the business name.
- Do not buy reviews or gate them.
- Do not create city-swapped pages with no local proof.
- Do not use a fake address or virtual office.
- Do not promise “#1 in Kolkata.”
