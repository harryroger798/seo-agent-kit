# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, be useful, be honest. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person. Do not submit fake reviews, invented data, or claims you cannot source.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone’s private data.
- Submitting secrets or client data.
- Fake reviews, fake case studies, or fabricated evidence.
- Spam, link schemes, or platform-policy evasion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate these rules. Report concerns to the maintainers via the contact in `SECURITY.md`.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct. It is not the full Contributor Covenant, but it follows the same spirit.
