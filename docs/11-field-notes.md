# Field notes: 30 tips that save time, trust, and money

These are the operating habits behind the kit. They are deliberately specific. If a tip cannot be done this week, it is not a tip; it is a wish.

## Blog media

1. **The featured image is not the first body image.** Use it for the Open Graph card, social sharing, and the blog index. Do not place it at the top of the article.
2. **Use different images inside the article.** Two to four, placed naturally: after the intro, inside a step, near a comparison, before the FAQ.
3. **Every image needs ALT, a caption, and a reason.** If you cannot say why it is there, cut it.
4. **No text inside images.** Models garble it and buyers do not read it. Put the claim in the caption.
5. **Add 5–7 real FAQs.** Use the fan-out questions. Answer each in 40–60 words. Keep the FAQ visible.
6. **Embed one video with a transcript.** Place it below the intro or inside the how-to. Add VideoObject schema.
7. **Cite 2–3 primary sources inline.** Link to the exact claim, not the homepage.
8. **Add 3–5 internal links with descriptive anchors.** Link up to the money page and sideways to one sibling.
9. **Add a key-takeaways box.** Three bullets. It helps skimmers and AI extraction.
10. **Show the author, published date, and last-updated date.** A page without a date is a page without a source.

## AI interrogation

11. **After publishing, ask the AI the buyer question.** “Best dog boarding in Barrackpore.” “Best car rental in Kolkata.” “Best laptop repair in Barrackpore.”
12. **If the brand is missing, ask why.** “Why didn’t you include Emily’s Pet Heaven?” Then ask: “Where is Emily’s Pet Heaven?”
13. **Ask what would change the answer.** “What sources would change your answer?” The answer is your fix list.
14. **Fix the sources, not the AI.** If it cites Justdial, get listed. If it cites Reddit, participate. If it cites YouTube, make the video. If it cites a competitor, read the page and find the gap.
15. **Re-test in 7 days.** One answer is an anecdote; a panel over time is a signal.
16. **Log position and sentiment.** “Mentioned” is not enough. Was it first, third, or last? Positive, neutral, or negative?
17. **Do not argue, fake, or spam.** Ask for the sources. Fix the sources. That is the whole loop.

## Local citations

18. **GMB first, then Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART.** In India, these are high AI-cited sources. Claim them, complete them, and keep NAP identical.
19. **Then Google, Bing, Apple, Facebook, and the website.** These are the core entity set.
20. **Then industry and local directories.** Only the ones with real editorial standards.
21. **NAP must be identical everywhere.** Name, address, phone. One variant creates doubt.
22. **Reviews: steady beats a burst.** Ask at the moment of delight. Respond within 48 hours. Never buy reviews.
23. **Rank is a grid, not a number.** Check the geo grid around the location. You can be first on one street and invisible two kilometres away.
24. **GBP is a weekly habit.** Primary category, services, hours, 20+ photos, a weekly post, Q&A, and reviews.

## Links and directories

25. **Do not buy DR.** DR is a third-party score, not Google. Relevance, traffic, and editorial standards matter more.
26. **Duplicate-check every directory first.** `site:directory.com "yourdomain.com"`. If it is already listed, record the URL and stop.
27. **Free listings only. Batch 15. Verify the email. Record the live URL. Re-check in 90 days.**
28. **A directory is a citation, not a strategy.** The top 30 relevant directories beat 687 random ones.

## Listings and paid

29. **A digital product listing is a trust document.** Answer what you get, how it arrives, how long it lasts, what happens if it fails, and whether you are authorised to sell it.
30. **Paid social is a conversation system.** One campaign, five creatives, one video, one WhatsApp flow. Measure cost per conversation, qualified rate, close rate, AOV, and ROAS. Clicks are not the goal.

## The one-line rules

- Cite or cut.
- One product link.
- Two checkpoints: brief, publish.
- One metric per sprint.
- Ship one thing a week.
- One source of truth: derivatives change the hook, not the facts.
- Do not promise #1. Promise proof.
