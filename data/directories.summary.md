## How to use

1. Start with Tier A, sorted by DR.
2. Duplicate-check each directory before submitting.
3. Verify the free tier, dofollow status, and editorial standard.
4. Submit in batches of 15.
5. Record the live URL only after the confirmation email is clicked.
6. Re-check after 90 days.

See `playbooks/directory-engine.md` and `checklists/directory-qa.md`.
